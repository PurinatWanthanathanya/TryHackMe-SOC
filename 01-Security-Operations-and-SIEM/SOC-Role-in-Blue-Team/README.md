# Lab: SOC Role in Blue Team

**Role:** Junior Security Analyst / SOC Analyst Trainee

## 🎯 Objective
To understand the organizational hierarchy of enterprise cybersecurity, differentiate the responsibilities of various Blue Team roles (L1/L2 Analysts, SOC Engineers, and CERT/CSIRT), and apply situational awareness to resolve real-world security incident scenarios.

## 🚀 Execution Steps
1. **Organizational Hierarchy & Role Mapping:** 
   * Categorized enterprise cybersecurity functions, distinguishing between Executive Leadership (CISO), Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), Offensive Security (Red Team), and Defensive Operations (Blue Team).
   * Mapped specific responsibilities within the Blue Team to understand the escalation flow between **Level 1 (Triage Analyst)**, **Level 2 (Incident Responder/Deep Investigator)**, and **SOC Engineers** (tool and SIEM maintenance).
2. **Operational Model Evaluation (Internal SOC vs. MSSP):** 
   * Evaluated the trade-offs between an **Internal SOC** (high organizational context and dedicated focus) and a Managed Security Service Provider (**MSSP**) (24/7 coverage, scalability, and specialized expertise).
   * Identified the distinct roles of Computer Emergency Response Teams (**CERT**) and Computer Security Incident Response Teams (**CSIRT**) during large-scale breaches.
3. **Executive Incident Decision-Making Challenge:** 
   * Assumed the role of CISO/Security Lead in a interactive simulation involving **7 distinct security incident scenarios**.
   * Analyzed each scenario to delegate tasks to the appropriate security units and make strategic decisions on containment, escalation, and remediation.

## 📊 Results & Evidence
* Successfully mapped cybersecurity organizational structures and completed the 7-scenario CISO decision-making challenge, correctly assigning operational roles and response workflows for diverse cyber threats.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
* **Clear Escalation Paths:** Learned that an efficient SOC relies on a well-defined escalation hierarchy—Level 1 analysts handle initial alert triage and false-positive filtering, allowing Level 2 analysts to focus on complex forensics and incident response.
* **Internal vs. Managed SOC:** Recognized that while an Internal SOC offers deeper integration with company culture and business logic, an MSSP provides cost-effective 24/7 monitoring and access to broader threat intelligence.
* **Incident Ownership (CERT/CSIRT):** Understood that while everyday alerts are managed within the SOC, critical or organization-wide emergencies require deployment of specialized teams like CERT/CSIRT for rapid containment and public/regulatory coordination.
* **Strategic Security Leadership:** Realized from the 7-scenario challenge that effective cybersecurity management involves not just technical mitigation, but also communication, GRC alignment, and assigning the right tasks to the right teams.

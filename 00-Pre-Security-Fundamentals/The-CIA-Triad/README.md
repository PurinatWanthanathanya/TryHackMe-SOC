# Lab: The CIA Triad

**Role:** Pre-Security Student / Information Security Trainee

## 🎯 Objective
To understand the fundamental principles of the CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability), evaluate security incidents based on their operational impact on data secrecy, accuracy, and system uptime, and apply this framework to establish robust defensive baselines across systems and networks.

## 🚀 Execution Steps
1. **Core Pillars & Security Mindset Analysis:** 
   * Deconstructed the three core pillars governing information security:
     * **Confidentiality:** Preventing unauthorized data access and exposure through access control mechanisms and encryption.
     * **Integrity:** Ensuring information remains trustworthy, authentic, and protected against unauthorized modification or tampering.
     * **Availability:** Guaranteeing reliable and timely access to critical systems, services, and data for authorized users.
2. **Security Incident Impact Assessment:** 
   * Utilized the CIA Triad framework to evaluate simulated security incidents, identifying whether attacks primarily targeted data confidentiality (e.g., data breaches), integrity (e.g., unauthorized file alterations), or availability (e.g., DoS/DDoS attacks).
   * Analyzed the interconnected nature of the three pillars, examining how compromises in one domain can impact organizational trust and system operations.
3. **Defensive Baseline & Risk Prioritization:** 
   * Applied the CIA Triad model to establish defensive baselines across operating systems and network infrastructures.
   * Prioritized system risks and evaluated preventive controls tailored to balance usability with appropriate security safeguards.

## 📊 Results & Evidence
* Successfully classified security threats under the three CIA dimensions, assessed operational impacts during incident simulations, and mapped preventive controls to preserve data integrity and service uptime.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
* **The Guiding North Star of Security:** Learned that every cybersecurity policy, firewall rule, and encryption standard is fundamentally implemented to support at least one pillar of the CIA Triad.
* **Incident Evaluation Lens:** Recognized that during an active incident triage, categorizing whether the threat affects confidentiality, integrity, or availability allows analysts to prioritize containment steps effectively.
* **Security as a Balancing Act:** Understood that maximizing all three pillars simultaneously presents trade-offs; organizations must align their defensive baselines according to specific business operations and risk tolerance.
